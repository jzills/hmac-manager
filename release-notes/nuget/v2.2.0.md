Policies can now come from configuration and change while your app runs.

- **Policies from configuration:** New `AddHmacManager` and `AddHmac` overloads take an `IConfigurationSection`, so you can define policies in `appsettings.json` or any other configuration provider.
- **Dynamic policies:** The `IHmacPolicyCollection` lets you add and remove policies at runtime.
- **Policies as JSON:** An `HmacPolicy` can be serialized and deserialized with `JsonSerializer`.
- **IntelliSense:** The XML documentation now renders in your editor.
